The Two Subcultures

Synthesis at the intellectual frontier

Max Ramsahoye

Within recent intellectual history, C.P. Snow’s The Two Cultures (1959) is considered a classic: representing an incisive intervention against a (Western) civilisation that had failed to properly integrate the sciences and the humanities, much to its detriment.

Any cursory observation of technological acceleration and its consequences – from crossing planetary boundaries to advancing AI – will show that this problem has persisted well into the 21st century, with the sciences (means or instrumentality) taking precedent over the humanities (ends or values).

When contemplating this frame, I have come to see a similar great divide arising at the frontier of contemporary theory outside of academia. I find myself positioned between two subcultures (or rather clusters of subcultures):

  • The PARA-academic space: encompassing progress studies, AI alignment, rationalism and effective altruism (also existential risk studies).
  • The Meta-space: oriented around the metacrisis, metamodernism and metatheory (also metarationality, metanarrative, metaphysics, metasystem, metacognition). Basically, if it has a meta- prefix it sounds cool and it counts..

Some within my intellectual circles — such as the emerging Integral Altruism movement — are aware of these two subcultures, though many further afield are not, despite their (arguably world-historical) significance. Further, my overall impression — and a premise of my writings here — is that these spaces have not sufficiently engaged with each other.

To the extent that there are those within each subculture that are aware of the other, there is little explicit mention or constructive interaction between the two; despite significant overlap in areas of interest and more interestingly, critical points of divergence.

As well as providing new conceptual handles for talking about this matter, in this article I’ll also briefly set out some of the affinities and antagonisms I see between the two subcultures.
